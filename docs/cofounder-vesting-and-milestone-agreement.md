## 3. Equity Earning Mechanisms (Vesting & Milestones)

The Co-Founder shall earn rights to purchase or receive ordinary shares through one or a combination of the following mechanisms:

### Option A: Standard Time-Based Vesting (4-Year Schedule with 1-Year Cliff)
- **1-Year Cliff:** No shares vest during the first 12 months of active contribution.
- **Month 12 (Cliff Date):** 25% of the allocated equity (37.5 shares / 3.75%) vests upon completing 12 consecutive months of verified active contribution.
- **Months 13–48:** The remaining 75% vests in equal monthly increments over the remaining 36 months (approx. 3.125 shares per month).

### Option B: Sales & Client Revenue Milestones (Performance Grants)
- **Milestone 1:** Earn **25 Shares (2.5% equity)** upon bringing and closing **KES 1,000,000** in verified client contracts paid to ImeanTech Ltd.
- **Milestone 2:** Earn an additional **25 Shares (2.5% equity)** upon reaching cumulative **KES 2,500,000** in closed client deals.
- **Milestone 3:** Earn an additional **50 Shares (5.0% equity)** upon reaching cumulative **KES 5,000,000** in closed client deals.
- **Cap:** Total equity earned through sales milestones shall not exceed 150 shares (15%).

---

## 4. Immediate Cash Referral Commission

In addition to equity milestones, the Co-Founder shall receive an immediate cash referral commission for client deals brought:
- **Commission Rate:** **7.5%** of the net contract revenue received by ImeanTech Ltd for any new client lead directly introduced and closed by the Co-Founder.
- **Payment Terms:** Payable within 14 days of ImeanTech Ltd receiving cleared client funds.

---

## 5. Termination & Bad Leaver Clauses

- **Unvested Shares:** If the Co-Founder ceases active involvement with ImeanTech Ltd for any reason, all unvested share options immediately expire and revert to the company pool.
- **Bad Leaver Clause:** If the Co-Founder is terminated for cause (breach of trust, fraud, working for a competitor, or non-performance), all unvested options and any previously vested shares shall be forfeited or subject to repurchase by ImeanTech Ltd at nominal par value (KES 100 per share).
- **IP Assignment:** All work, leads, ideas, software, and clients introduced under this arrangement remain the exclusive intellectual property of **ImeanTech Ltd**.
