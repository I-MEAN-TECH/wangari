# Wangari — "Trading As" Disclosure & Registration Notes

> **Purpose:** the exact wording to use on pitch materials, contracts, and the BRS business-name filing, so the ImeanTech Ltd ↔ Wangari relationship is always stated plainly instead of discovered.

---

## 1. The disclosure line (use everywhere)

On the one-pager, pitch deck, LOIs, and email signature, state it once, plainly:

> **"Wangari Farm OS is built and operated by ImeanTech Ltd (Nairobi, Kenya)."**

Short form for a footer or signature block:

> *Wangari Farm OS — a product of ImeanTech Ltd*

Do not let a partner or investor find this out later. Stating it upfront reads as organised; being asked about it in diligence reads as a surprise.

## 2. Registering "Wangari" as a business name at BRS

Kenya's Business Registration Service (`brs.go.ke`, filed via `ecitizen.go.ke`) allows a registered company to file a **business name** — a "trading as" registration distinct from the company's legal name. Steps:

1. Log into the eCitizen BRS portal with the ImeanTech Ltd account (needs the company's Certificate of Incorporation and KRA PIN first).
2. Search and reserve the name **"Wangari"** or **"Wangari Farm OS"** as a business name.
3. File the business name registration naming **ImeanTech Ltd** as the proprietor.
4. Pay the filing fee online (a few thousand KES — materially cheaper and faster than a full company registration).
5. Receive the business name certificate: *"ImeanTech Ltd, trading as Wangari."*

This certificate is the document to show a co-op, county, or investor who asks "is this registered?" — it is small, fast, and removes any ambiguity.

## 3. Registering the trademark at KIPI

Separately (and more importantly, long-term): file the **name "Wangari"/"Wangari Farm OS" and the logo** as a trademark with the Kenya Industrial Property Institute (`kipi.go.ke`, Trade Marks service, payable via the KIPI eCitizen portal).

- Kenya is **first-to-file** — whoever files first gets the mark, regardless of who used it first. File before wider public launch (i.e., before or immediately around AIAE 2026).
- File in the name of **ImeanTech Ltd**, not the founder personally — this keeps it consistent with the IP ownership memo.
- Relevant trademark classes to consider: software/SaaS (Class 9 and/or 42 for software services), and possibly Class 35/36 if positioning touches financial/record-keeping services. A patent/trademark agent can confirm the right class(es) — KIPI publishes a Patent Agents list for this.

## 4. What this does and doesn't solve

- **Solves:** name clarity, a document to show partners, first-to-file protection on the brand.
- **Doesn't solve:** it does not create a separate legal entity for Wangari. If you later want Wangari to have its own cap table (e.g., before a priced equity round with outside investors), that requires a separate incorporation and an asset-transfer agreement moving the code/brand/contracts from ImeanTech Ltd into the new entity. Not needed now — revisit only when a priced round is actually on the table.
