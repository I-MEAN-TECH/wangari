# Wangari — Pilot Letter of Intent (LOI) Template

> **Purpose:** the one-page document to get signed at AIAE 2026 with a co-operative, feed company, or county extension office. This is the asset — more valuable than any investor conversation on its own (see `aiae-2026-readiness.md` §6).
> **Use:** print 3–5 copies before the event. Fill in the blanks by hand or beforehand if a meeting is pre-booked. An LOI is non-binding — it is a statement of serious intent, not a contract, so it is fast to get signed.
