## 3. Can we come out with investors? The honest split

| Claim | Truth |
|---|---|
| "We will leave AIAE with a **funded cheque**" | **No.** Not on 9 users / 0 revenue / no team / no tests. A cheque at this stage would require either a relationship already in motion, or an investor who funds on team alone — we have one founder and no revenue to de-risk it. |
| "We will leave AIAE with **investor interest, a named contact, and a defined next milestone**" | **Yes — this is achievable and is the right goal.** O2 explicitly exists to attract financiers into the room. A 15-minute conversation that ends in *"come back when you have a signed pilot"* is a **win**, not a rejection. |
| "We will leave AIAE with **a pilot partner** (co-op / feed company / county)" | **Yes, most likely outcome.** This is the real currency, because it is the thing that *unblocks* the investor conversation. |

**The reframe:** the objective is not "get money at AIAE." It is **"convert one of the 200+ exhibitors who each control hundreds of farmers into a signed pilot, and get two investors to name the milestone that unlocks them."** The pilot is the asset; the investor conversation is a by-product.

## 5. "Solve all the available problems in one platform" — read this carefully

This is a **product vision** (and [vision.md](vision.md) is its charter). It is **already half-true and provable**: the crops table holds coffee, tea, onions, macadamia, avocado; the livestock side holds poultry and dairy. Whole-farm is real and it is our genuine differentiator against poultry-only Kenyan apps.

But as an **investor line, "we solve all the problems" is a liability**, for three reasons:

1. **Investors fund a wedge, not a platform.** "All problems" reads as "no focus" — the single most common reason first-time founders are passed on.
2. **Breadth without adoption has no value.** [gap-analysis.md](gap-analysis.md) says it plainly: *"A bank will not lend against a record the farmer never wrote."* All twelve problem-domains we could cover are worth nothing until one farmer records one litre of milk and sees money.
3. **It invites scope you cannot defend.** Saying we solve logistics, insurance, credit, traceability, market access and advisory in one app invites the question "which of those can you actually deliver this quarter?" — and the honest answer is none at full depth.

**The defensible version of the same ambition** — keep this, it is true and it is a moat:

> **"One platform, one wedge: we make the farmer record and see money — and because every new capability attaches to a table that already exists ([module-plan.md](module-plan.md): EXTEND NEVER REBUILD), we add problem-domains at the cost of a screen, not a rewrite."**

That sentence converts "we do everything" (a red flag) into "we have an architecture that lets us do everything, and we chose to start with money truth" (a strategy). Same vision, opposite investor reaction.

**Category name to use at AIAE:** *whole-farm record + money truth for Kenyan smallholders.* Not "farm OS for everything."

## 6. Definition of success — write this on a card before you go

AIAE was a success if, by 28 Oct, we have:

1. **One signed pilot LOI or MOU** with a co-op, feed company or county (the asset).
2. **Two named investor/funder contacts** with a **specific next milestone** agreed in writing.
3. **One grant or support-unit channel** opened (Ministry digital unit, or a development partner).
4. **Zero inflated claims made.** 9 users, 0 revenue — said calmly, every time.

Anything less than (1) means the three days produced contacts, not progress. Anything more than (1) in one expo is optimism.

## 7. Deal-breakers (the rules that protect the outcome)

- **Never inflate.** The honesty *is* the pitch — anyone can open the app in 5 minutes and see it is real and good.
- **Go to meet, not to sell.** Goal: *meet three people who matter, leave with one booked follow-up.*
- **Don't claim ANITRAC as live** (0 rows). Don't claim "all problems solved." Don't claim users you don't have.
- **Name the enemy, not competitors** ([vision.md](vision.md)): the enemy is guesswork and the notebook.
- **Follow up within 24 hours.** A contact without a follow-up is a business card in a pocket.
