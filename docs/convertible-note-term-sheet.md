# Wangari — Convertible Note Term Sheet (draft, for discussion only)

> **Purpose:** a ready answer if an investor at AIAE 2026 asks "what terms are you raising on?" This is a **discussion draft**, not a binding offer — have a lawyer review before signing anything. Kenya does not have a standardised "SAFE" instrument, so the equivalent here is a **convertible loan agreement**, which Kenyan lawyers and investors already use and understand.
> **Philosophy (see the funding-strategy discussion):** keep this small, defer the valuation argument until there's revenue, and don't accept venture-scale terms for a durable/profitable company. See `valuation-audit.md` for the honest numbers this is based on.

---

## Indicative terms

| Term | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument | Convertible loan agreement (interest-bearing loan that converts to equity at a future priced round) | Kenya's closest equivalent to a US SAFE; standard, lawyer-recognised, fast to draft |
| Amount | KES 500,000 – 3,000,000 per investor (indicative range) | Enough to fund 3–6 months of focused distribution work (partnerships, onboarding support, one hire); not a venture-scale raise |
| Valuation cap | KES 15,000,000 – 25,000,000 (indicative, for discussion) | Anchored to cost-to-rebuild (~KES 3–8M, see `valuation-audit.md`) plus a reasonable premium for the working product, test suite, and distribution mechanism — not a story multiple |
| Discount | 15–20% to the next priced round | Standard compensation for investing before a priced round exists |
| Interest | 5–8% per annum, simple, accruing until conversion | Standard for Kenyan convertible loan agreements; protects the investor if conversion is delayed |
| Maturity | 18–24 months | If no priced round happens by then, the note either converts at the cap or is repaid/renegotiated |
| Conversion trigger | A future priced equity round of at least KES 10,000,000 | Protects against a trivial raise triggering conversion at the cap |
| Most-favoured-nation (MFN) clause | Yes | If a later investor gets better terms before conversion, this note gets them too |
| Use of funds (be specific when asked) | Partnership onboarding support, one part-time hire (ops or sales), continued hosting/infra, pilot execution costs | Investors fund a plan, not a vibe — have this ready |

## What this deliberately avoids

- **No priced equity round yet.** Pricing the company today, at 13 users and KES 0 revenue, locks in a number neither side can defend. A convertible note defers that argument to a point where there's real data.
- **No board seat or control terms at this size.** A KES 500K–3M note from one angel does not warrant a board seat; keep it simple. If a larger, led round appears later, that's a different document.
- **No large single check assumed.** This is deliberately sized to extend runway and prove the next milestone (per `aiae-2026-readiness.md`), not to fund a venture-scale growth sprint the company isn't ready for.

## Before signing anything

1. Get a Kenyan corporate/commercial lawyer to review and adapt this to ImeanTech Ltd's actual incorporation documents (not a template substitute for legal advice).
2. Confirm the note is between the investor and **ImeanTech Ltd** (the entity), consistent with `legal-ip-ownership-memo.md`.
3. Keep a simple running cap table from day one — even one line ("Lewis Ndungu — 100%") — so the first note's conversion math is easy to verify later.
