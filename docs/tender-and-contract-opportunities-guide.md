# ImeanTech Ltd — Tenders, Contracts & Business Opportunities Guide (Kenya & Africa)

> **Purpose:** Strategic guide detailing where **ImeanTech Ltd** can source, apply for, and win public tenders, private corporate contracts, and international development projects in Kenya and across East Africa.

## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Target Sectors

ImeanTech Ltd's broad scope covers four major revenue-generating pillars:
1. **Custom Software & Web Systems:** Enterprise portals, CRMs, cloud SaaS, mobile apps.
2. **AgriTech & IoT Systems (Wangari):** Co-op milk collection, livestock tracking, cold-chain monitoring, soil sensors.

## 🏛️ CATEGORY 1: Kenya Public Sector Tenders (National & County)

By law (Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act), all Kenyan public entities must publish tenders online and reserve **30% of opportunities for Youth, Women, and PWDs (AGPO)**.

### 1. Official Procurement Portals to Register & Bookmark:
- **Public Procurement Information Portal (PPIP):** `tenders.go.ke`
  - Central portal where all ministries, state corporations, and counties publish active tender notices.
- **e-GP System (Electronic Government Procurement):** `egpkenya.go.ke`
  - End-to-end e-tendering system. Register your company profile here to bid electronically.
- **AGPO Portal (Affirmative Action):** `agpo.go.ke`
  - Apply for the **Youth AGPO Certificate** (Free). Allows ImeanTech Ltd to bid on tenders reserved exclusively for youth-owned companies (no heavy financial history required).

### 2. Primary Government Agencies to Target:
- **ICT Authority (ICTA):** `icta.go.ke`
  - Tenders for the *Digital Superhighway*, public Wi-Fi, government cloud, software licensing, and digital skills.
- **Ministry of Agriculture & Livestock Development:** `kilimo.go.ke`
  - Tenders for farmer registration platforms, livestock tracking systems, fertilizer e-voucher portals, and value-chain digitisation.
- **State Agencies & Agricultural Boards:**
  - **KALRO** (Kenya Agricultural & Livestock Research Organization)
  - **Kenya Dairy Board (KDB)**
  - **National Irrigation Authority (NIA)**
  - **Kenya Tea Development Agency (KTDA)**
- **47 County Governments:**
  - Counties (e.g., Nairobi, Kiambu, Nakuru, Nyeri, Murang'a, Kirinyaga, Uasin Gishu) routinely publish tenders for:
    - County revenue collection software & hospital management systems.
    - Agricultural value-chain digitisation & milk collection centers.
    - ICT networking, structured cabling, and hardware maintenance for county offices.

## 🌍 CATEGORY 2: International Organizations & UN Agencies (Kenya HQ & Africa)

Kenya hosts major UN agency regional headquarters and international NGOs with large IT and agriculture budgets.

### 1. UN Global Marketplace (UNGM):
- **Portal:** `ungm.org` (Register company at **Basic Level** — Free).
- **Target UN Agencies Operating in Kenya/East Africa:**
  - **FAO** (Food and Agriculture Organization): Digital agriculture & livestock data systems.
  - **WFP** (World Food Programme): Food chain tracking & smallholder farmer software.
  - **UNEP** & **UN-Habitat** (Nairobi HQ): Web applications, data dashboards, managed IT support.
  - **IFAD** (International Fund for Agricultural Development): Agri-tech innovation tenders.

### 2. World Bank & African Development Bank (AfDB):
- **World Bank Kenya Projects:** `projects.worldbank.org`
  - Projects like *Kenya Digital Economy Acceleration Project* and *DRIVE (De-risking, Inclusion and Value Enhancement of Pastoral Economies)*.
- **African Development Bank Procurement:** `afdb.org`
  - Regional Pan-African tenders for digital agriculture, youth tech initiatives, and ICT infrastructure.

### 3. International NGOs & Donors in East Africa:
- **USAID Kenya / Feed the Future:** Funding partners digitising agriculture and youth employment.
- **AGRA (Alliance for a Green Revolution in Africa):** Grants and tech contracts for smallholder farmer solutions.
- **Mercy Corps Kenya & FSD Kenya (Financial Sector Deepening):** Digital identity, informal worker inclusion, and SACCO digitisation.

## 🏢 CATEGORY 3: Private Sector & Co-operative Contracts

Private sector contracts offer faster procurement cycles and immediate cash flow without public tender delays.

### 1. Agricultural Co-operatives & SACCOs (Immediate Target for Wangari):
- **Dairy Co-operatives:**
  - Examples: *Githunguri Dairy (Fresha), New KCC, Mukurweini Dairy, Wakulima Dairy, Kiambaa Dairy*.
  - Contract opportunities: Milk intake digitisation, solar cold-room monitoring, farmer ledger software, automated SMS payouts.
- **Coffee & Tea Cooperative Unions:**
  - Factory weighbridge integration, cherry delivery tracking systems, SACCO advance loan systems.

### 2. Corporate IT & Networking Contracts (ImeanTech Ltd Core):
- **Commercial Property & Office Buildings:** Structured cabling, Wi-Fi installation, CCTV/networking setup for offices.
- **SMEs & Corporate Businesses:** Website design, custom CRM/ERP development, managed IT support contracts (retainers of KES 50,000–300,000/month).
- **Sub-Contracting with Tier-1 Systems Integrators:** Partnering as software/field implementation sub-contractor for large ICT firms bidding on multi-million KES tenders.

## 📋 SECTION 4: Prerequisites Checklist to Bid & Win Tenders

To ensure your bid is never disqualified at Preliminary Evaluation:

### 1. Statutory Documents (Mandatory for All Public Tenders):
- [ ] **Certificate of Incorporation** (`ImeanTech Limited`)
- [ ] **CR12 Certificate** (Issued within last 6 months)
- [ ] **Company KRA PIN Certificate** (`ImeanTech Limited`)
- [ ] **Valid Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC)** (Renewable yearly on KRA iTax)
- [ ] **AGPO Youth Certificate** (Applied online at `agpo.go.ke`)
- [ ] **Business Permit** (Nairobi County / Local Single Business Permit)
- [ ] **NSSF & SHIF/NHIF Employer Certificates** (Register company as employer)

### 2. Technical & Financial Pitch Documents:
- [ ] **Professional Company Profile** (PDF listing services, tech stack, team, Waris Mall office).
- [ ] **Audited Accounts or Certified Bank Statements** (Bank statements suffice for AGPO youth tenders).
- [ ] **Reference Letters / Portfolio** (Past custom systems built, e.g., Wangari, Workora, web projects).
