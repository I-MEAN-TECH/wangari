# Wangari — IP Ownership Memo (internal record)

> **Purpose:** a short, dated statement of who owns what. Not a public document — keep it in `docs/` so it travels with the repo, and reference it if a partner, investor, or lawyer asks "who owns Wangari?"
> **Status:** internal record, to be re-confirmed in the ImeanTech Ltd statutory records once incorporation completes.

---

## 1. The entities

- **ImeanTech Ltd** — the legal entity (registered/registering in Kenya under the Companies Act, 2015).
- **Wangari** (also "Wangari Farm OS") — a product and brand, **not a separate legal person**. It is owned and operated by ImeanTech Ltd.

## 2. What ImeanTech Ltd owns

As of the date below, ImeanTech Ltd owns, by virtue of being the operating company and sole trading entity of its founder, Lewis Ndungu:

| Asset | Description |
|---|---|
| Source code | All code in the `wangari-next/` and `server/` repositories, including all commits, branches, and history |
| Trademark / brand | The name "Wangari" / "Wangari Farm OS" and associated logo, wordmark, and visual identity |
| Domain | `wangari.imeantech.com` and any other Wangari-branded subdomains or domains registered by the founder |
| Data | The production database, all user/farm records, and all backups |
| Contracts | Any Paystack, SMTP, hosting, VPS, and third-party service agreements signed in connection with Wangari |
| Pre-incorporation work | All code and assets created by Lewis Ndungu before ImeanTech Ltd's incorporation date are hereby assigned to ImeanTech Ltd as of the date of incorporation (standard founder IP assignment — see §4) |

## 3. Why this memo exists

An investor, pilot partner, or acquirer doing even light diligence will ask "who legally owns the Wangari IP?" Before this memo, the honest answer was "the founder, informally." This memo — and the matching clause to be added to ImeanTech Ltd's statutory records once incorporated — makes the answer "ImeanTech Ltd, formally," which is what every subsequent legal document (investment agreement, pilot LOI, trademark filing) needs to be true.

## 4. Founder IP assignment (do this at incorporation)

When ImeanTech Ltd is incorporated, add a short **IP Assignment Agreement** between Lewis Ndungu (as an individual) and ImeanTech Ltd, assigning all pre-incorporation Wangari IP (code, brand, domain, data) to the company in exchange for founder shares. This is standard practice — most startups do this in their first board resolution — and it closes the only real gap between "I built this" and "the company owns this."

A one-paragraph version, to be signed by the founder and the company:

> *I, Lewis Ndungu, hereby assign to ImeanTech Ltd all right, title, and interest in the Wangari Farm OS software, brand, trademark, domain names, and related intellectual property created by me prior to the date of incorporation, in consideration for the ordinary shares issued to me in ImeanTech Ltd.*

## 5. Trademark filing (separate action — see `trading-as-disclosure.md`)

Filing "Wangari" as a trademark at KIPI, in ImeanTech Ltd's name, is the step that converts this memo's claim into a registered, enforceable right. Kenya is first-to-file — do this before any wider public launch or before a competitor files first.
